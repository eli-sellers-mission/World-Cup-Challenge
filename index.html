How the win projections work
The projections come from a Monte Carlo simulation — the rest of the tournament is played out thousands of times (1,000 / 3,000 / 8,000 sims) from the results so far, and the outcomes are averaged.
  • Locked: completed group matches and any knockout picks already made are held fixed in every simulation.
  • Unplayed group games: goals are drawn from a Poisson model whose expected goals come from the two teams' power ratings (editable above).
  • Knockout games: the winner is chosen by an Elo win-probability based on those same ratings.
  • Outputs: how often each team advances, how often each team wins the title, and how often each drafter finishes #1 (ties are split evenly).
These are a transparent model you can tune via the power ratings — not betting odds.
How scoring works — Option A
A drafter's total is the sum of all points earned by the teams they drafted.
  • Group stage: Win +3, Draw +1 (each team), Loss +0.
  • Advancement: +3 for each team that reaches the knockout round — the top two of its group, or one of the eight best third-place teams.
  • Knockout wins (bonus to the team that wins the match): Round of 32 +4, Round of 16 +6, Quarterfinal +8, Semifinal +10, Final +14.
  • Third-place playoff: win +3.
